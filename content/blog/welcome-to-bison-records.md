Bison Records started the way most things worth starting do — with a conversation that went too long and a handshake that meant something.

We're not trying to be everything to everyone. We're a small operation with a specific taste and a lot of patience. The artists we work with make music that asks something of the listener. That's the only criterion that matters to us.

## What We're Building

We're building a home for music that doesn't fit neatly into a playlist algorithm. The kind of record you put on and leave alone. The kind of song that means something different the third time you hear it.

Our roster is small by design. We'd rather do a few things well than many things adequately.

## What's Coming

Our first release — *Year Of The Brown Bear* by Nikolas Murdock — comes out November 5th. We've been sitting with this record for months and we're proud of it in the quiet way that matters.

More artists, more music, more soon.

Stay close.

— Bison Records
